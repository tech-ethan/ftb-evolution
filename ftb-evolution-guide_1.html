Getting Started & Adventure

The pack is built for exploring as much as building. These are the places, helper mods and habits that make the journey smoother.

Your first day

  1. Open the quest book and read the first chapter. Quests hand out loot crates and point you at the next step.
  2. Punch trees, make stone tools, then a Silent Gear pickaxe as soon as you have iron.
  3. Build a small shelter with a bed and a few chests. Craft a Sophisticated Backpack early.
  4. Mine down for iron, copper, osmium and tin. The world goes down to Y −128, so deep caves are rich but dangerous.
  5. Set up starter power (a coal or fuel generator) and your first Mekanism machines.
  6. Find a Waystone near spawn so you can get home quickly later.

Helper mods worth learning

Waystones

Activate Waystones you find, then teleport between them. Place your own at home.

Mystical Agriculture

Grow resources as crops. Start with Inferium Essence from Inferium ore, then craft seeds for iron, gold and more.

Productive Bees

Catch bees that produce ore combs. A good passive source of metals.

Pufffish Skills

A skill tree that levels as you play. Find its key in Controls and spend points often.

Loot crates

Quest rewards and dungeon loot come in crates. Open them early; they hold useful gear.

Farmer's Delight

Cooked meals restore more hunger and give buffs like Comfort and Nourishment.

Occultism & Forbidden Arcanus

More magic: spirits that work for you, and powerful rituals. Good side paths when you want variety.

XNet / Integrated Dynamics

Advanced logistics. Use them for complex item and fluid routing once your base grows.

Survival and combat habits

  • Carry food you can eat on the move. Farmer's Delight meals give longer-lasting buffs than plain food.
  • Hold a shield in your off-hand until you have good armor. Apotheosis bosses hit far harder than normal mobs.
  • Before a big trip, set your bed or a Waystone and empty your backpack into storage.
  • Light up your base area. Some dimensions and deep caves spawn tougher mobs.
  • Put Silent Gear repair kits or Renew-trait materials on tools you can't afford to lose.
  • Test a new weapon build on regular mobs before you spend rare materials on it.

Keys and screens to know

JEI

Hover an item: R shows recipes, U shows uses. Search with @modname to list a mod's items.

Controls menu

Open Options → Controls → Key Binds to find keys for the skill tree, MekaSuit modes, backpack and spell casting.

Quest book

Quests show the pack's intended path and unlock rewards. Check it whenever you feel stuck.

Endgame goal

The FTB Pyramid is the pack's quest-driven endgame. It asks for items from across every mod and rewards powerful creative items, so everything in this guide feeds into it.

Tech & Power
Mekanism (+ Tools, Generators)Medium → Hard5/5

First goal: an Enrichment Chamber, which turns 1 ore into 2 dusts.

Getting started

  1. Mine Osmium ore in the Overworld and smelt it into ingots. Grab tin, copper and lead while you are down there.
  2. Set up a small power source first (a coal or fuel generator, or Mekanism's Heat Generator next to lava).
  3. Craft a Metallurgic Infuser. Load it with carbon (coal or charcoal) and iron to make Enriched Iron.
  4. Infuse Enriched Iron with more carbon to get Steel dust, then smelt it into Steel ingots.
  5. Craft Steel Casings. Almost every Mekanism machine needs one.
  6. Build an Enrichment Chamber: ore → 2 dusts → smelt. This is your 2x ore line.
  7. Make an Energized Smelter and a Crusher so your line runs on power instead of fuel.
  8. Craft Basic Universal Cables to move power and Logistical Transporters to move items between machines.
  9. Add an Electrolytic Separator on water for Oxygen and Hydrogen. Oxygen powers the 3x line.
  10. Build a Purification Chamber + Chemical Injection Chamber for 3x and 4x ore, then the Chemical Dissolution Chamber + Washer + Crystallizer for 5x.
  11. Store power in Energy Cubes, then an Induction Matrix (multiblock) once you have lithium.
  12. Mid-game power: Ethylene in Gas-Burning Generators, or Wind and Solar generators for passive power.
  13. Build a Thermal Evaporation Plant on brine for lithium, needed for Induction Cells.
  14. Endgame: build a Fission Reactor (with safety cut-offs), process nuclear waste into polonium, and craft the MekaSuit.
  15. Final step: a Fusion Reactor for huge power, and the SPS for antimatter.

Tips

  • Speed and Energy upgrades go in any machine. Stack them.
  • The Configurator changes which side of a machine takes input and gives output; colored sides match the machine's GUI.
  • Factory upgrades turn a single machine into a 3-, 5-, 7- or 9-slot factory.
  • Never run a Fission Reactor without a way to remove waste and heat. Wire a Fission Reactor Logic Adapter to shut it down when it gets hot.
  • A Digital Miner can mine whole areas for you once it has power and filters.
  • Mekanism's Teleporter and Portable Teleporter save hours of travel.

Guide: No in-game book. Use JEI, and hover machines while holding Shift for details.

Immersive EngineeringEasy–Medium4/5

First goal: a Coke Oven for coal coke, then a Blast Furnace for steel.

Getting started

  1. Craft an Engineer's Hammer. You right-click a finished multiblock frame with it to form the machine.
  2. Make Coke Bricks (clay + sandstone + bricks) and build a 3x3x3 Coke Oven.
  3. Burn coal in the Coke Oven for Coal Coke and Creosote Oil (save the creosote for treated wood).
  4. Craft Blast Bricks and build a Blast Furnace. Iron + coal coke → steel.
  5. Make Treated Wood (planks + creosote) for windmills and water wheels.
  6. Place a Water Wheel in flowing water or a Windmill in open air, connected to a Kinetic Dynamo.
  7. Craft LV Wire Coils and LV connectors. Connect the dynamo to a Capacitor and your machines.
  8. Build a Crusher (multiblock) for ore doubling and an Arc Furnace later for ore tripling and alloys.
  9. Set up a Fermenter + Squeezer + Refinery to make Biodiesel from crops and seeds.
  10. Run Diesel Generators (multiblock) on Biodiesel for strong mid-game power.
  11. Upgrade wiring to MV and HV as power use grows. Use Transformers between voltages.
  12. Late game: the Excavator mines endless mineral veins underground; use the Core Sample Drill to find them.

Tips

  • Windmills need open space around them to spin.
  • Wires need the right voltage connector, or they burn out.
  • A Garden Cloche grows crops automatically with power and water.
  • The Revolver and Railgun are strong ranged weapons if you want to try them.
  • Conveyor belts and Item Routers make cheap early sorting.

Guide: Craft the Engineer's Manual (book + lever). It has every multiblock layout.

OritechMedium4/5

First goal: a Basic Generator and a Pulverizer.

Getting started

  1. Gather copper, iron and gold; Oritech's early recipes use all three.
  2. Craft a Basic Generator and burn fuel for your first power.
  3. Build a Pulverizer for ore doubling and a Powered Furnace for smelting.
  4. Craft Energy Pipes and Item Pipes to link your machines.
  5. Unlock the Assembler and Foundry for Oritech's alloys (like Electrum and Adamant) and machine parts.
  6. Build a Centrifuge and Atomic Forge as they unlock in the quest book.
  7. Move up to Steam and Bio generators for steady mid-game power.
  8. Add Machine Addons next to machines for speed, efficiency and capacity.
  9. Set up a Laser Arm and drone port for automated mining and long-range item transport.
  10. Craft the Exoskeleton armor and jetpack for mid-game mobility.

Tips

  • Oritech machines accept add-on blocks placed next to them for speed and efficiency.
  • Its exoskeleton armor is a solid mid-game alternative before the MekaSuit.
  • Check machine GUIs for an addon slot count; bigger machines take more addons.

Guide: Use JEI and the quest book chapter for Oritech.

Industrial ForegoingMedium4/5

First goal: rubber → plastic, then machine frames.

Getting started

  1. Plant a small tree farm (any logs work, rubber trees are best).
  2. Place a Fluid Extractor against a tree log to collect latex, with power connected.
  3. Turn latex into Dryrubber in the Latex Processing Unit, then smelt Dryrubber into Plastic.
  4. Craft a Simple Machine Frame. Most IF machines need one.
  5. Build a Plant Gatherer and Plant Sower to automate your tree farm.
  6. Set up a Biofuel line: crops → Bioreactor → Biofuel → Biofuel Generator.
  7. Build mob farms: a Mob Duplicator with Mob Imprisonment Tool + a Mob Crusher or Slaughter Factory.
  8. Collect Pink Slime from the Slaughter Factory's output for higher-tier parts.
  9. Craft Advanced and Supreme Machine Frames as you need stronger machines.
  10. Build a Laser Drill to pull ores from nothing, using a matching laser lens.
  11. Craft Infinity tools (Drill, Saw, Hammer, Trident, Backpack) and charge them with FE.

Tips

  • Range Addons make farms and harvesters cover a bigger area.
  • Infinity tools gain tiers as you put more power into them.
  • The Mob Crusher collects mob XP as fluid essence, great for Apotheosis enchanting and mending.
  • Speed and Efficiency addons cut machine power use.

Guide: Use JEI; each machine explains its addons in its tooltip.

Modern IndustrializationHard4/5

First goal: a Bronze Boiler powering steam machines.

Getting started

  1. Mix copper and tin into bronze, then craft bronze plates and gears.
  2. Build a Bronze Boiler and feed it fuel and water for steam.
  3. Use steam machines: Bronze Macerator, Compressor, Furnace, Mixer.
  4. Pipe steam with Fluid Pipes from the boiler to each machine.
  5. Build a Coke Oven multiblock for coke, then a Steam Blast Furnace for steel.
  6. Upgrade to steel boilers and steel machines for faster work.
  7. Move to the electric age: build a Steam Turbine and LV electric machines with EU cables.
  8. Set up an Electric Blast Furnace for better alloys like aluminum and stainless steel.
  9. Build large multiblocks: Large Steam Boiler, Oil Drilling Rig, Distillation Tower.
  10. Late game: large Diesel Generators and Nuclear Reactors.

Tips

  • MI uses EU, not FE. Plan converters if you mix it with other mods.
  • Multiblock previews show where each block goes. Use them.
  • Hatches on multiblocks have their own tiers; match them to your energy tier.

Guide: Has an in-game guidebook. Read the Steam Age chapter first.

Actually AdditionsEasy3.5/5

First goal: a Coal Generator, then a Crusher.

Getting started

  1. Craft the manual first; it unlocks pages as you craft items.
  2. Craft a Coal Generator for early power.
  3. Build a Crusher for ore doubling (the Double Crusher handles two ores at once).
  4. Plant Canola. Press it in a Canola Press for Canola Oil.
  5. Burn Canola Oil in an Oil Generator, or upgrade it to Refined Canola Oil in a Fermenting Barrel.
  6. Craft an Atomic Reconstructor. Point it at blocks to turn them into crystal materials.
  7. Make Restonia, Palis, Diamatine, Void, Emeradic and Enori crystals with the Reconstructor.
  8. Build an Empowerer to make Empowered crystals for higher-tier items.
  9. Use Laser Relays to send power and items without cables.
  10. Craft Phantom Faces and Item Interfaces for simple remote logistics.

Tips

  • Laser Relays move power and items without cables.
  • Item interfaces and Phantom blocks are cheap logistics early.
  • The Farmer block plants and harvests crops automatically.

Guide: Craft the Actually Additions Manual (book + paper). It is detailed.

Ender IOMedium3.5/5

First goal: an Alloy Smelter.

Getting started

  1. Craft a basic Capacitor; most Ender IO machines need one.
  2. Build an Alloy Smelter to make alloys like Conductive Alloy and Energetic Alloy.
  3. Make a SAG Mill for ore doubling. Add Grinding Balls for bonus output.
  4. Craft Stirling Generators and burn fuel for power.
  5. Craft Energy Conduits, Item Conduits and Fluid Conduits.
  6. Stack several conduit types in one block to save space.
  7. Upgrade machine capacitors (Double-Layer, Octadic) for speed and efficiency.
  8. Build a Combustion Generator for stronger power from fuels.
  9. Add a Powered Spawner or Soul Binder later for mob-related automation.

Tips

  • One conduit block can carry power, items and fluids at once.
  • Use a Yeta Wrench to configure conduit connections.
  • Item conduit filters let you sort items without extra blocks.

Guide: Use JEI and tooltips; Shift shows extra machine info.

RFTools PowerEasy3/5

First goal: a Dimensional Cell to store power.

Getting started

  1. Craft a Coal Generator for basic power.
  2. Craft Dimensional Cells and place them touching each other; they act as one battery.
  3. Set each cell face to input or output.
  4. Use Power Cells and a Power Card to share power between distant bases.
  5. Late game: try the Endergenic Generator for big power with careful pearl timing.

Tips

  • Dimensional Cells are a cheap battery bank for any FE mod.
  • Advanced cells hold much more; upgrade before your base grows.

Guide: Use JEI; tooltips explain each block.

Storage
Refined StorageEasy–Medium4.5/5

First goal: Controller + Disk Drive + Grid with a 1k disk.

Getting started

  1. Follow the build order in the Storage section of this guide to get the Controller online.
  2. Add a Crafting Grid so you can craft straight from storage.
  3. Upgrade storage with 4k, 16k and 64k disks as you fill up.
  4. Add an External Storage block on chests or barrels so they show up in the Grid.
  5. Add Importers and Exporters with filters to move items to and from machines.
  6. Make a Pattern Grid and write Patterns for things you craft often.
  7. Place Patterns in an Autocrafter next to the network for autocrafting.
  8. Use processing patterns for machine recipes (ore → dust → ingot).
  9. Add a Wireless Transmitter and craft a Wireless Grid to reach storage anywhere in range.
  10. Use Detectors and Constructors/Destructors for automation tricks.

Tips

  • Use filters on Importers so they don't pull everything.
  • Upgrade to bigger disks before the network fills up; full disks stop accepting items.
  • Keep a backup power source so the network never shuts off.
  • Put Speed Upgrades in Importers and Exporters for fast transfers.

Guide: Use JEI and the Refined Storage section of this guide.

Sophisticated Storage / BackpacksEasy4/5

First goal: a leather backpack with a Pickup upgrade.

Getting started

  1. Craft a basic backpack from leather and a chest.
  2. Wear it (it goes in your chest slot or a curio slot) or carry it.
  3. Add a Pickup upgrade so items go straight in.
  4. Upgrade the backpack tier: iron → gold → diamond → netherite.
  5. Add a Magnet upgrade to pull in items around you.
  6. Add a Feeding upgrade so it eats for you when you are hungry.
  7. Add Compacting and Void upgrades to compress and delete junk.
  8. Craft Sophisticated chests and barrels and upgrade their tiers the same way.
  9. Link chests with a Storage Controller, or connect them to Refined Storage.

Tips

  • A Feeding upgrade eats for you automatically.
  • A Void upgrade with a filter deletes junk like cobblestone.
  • A Tank upgrade lets your backpack carry fluids.
  • Crafting and Smithing upgrades let you craft from inside the backpack.

Guide: Use JEI; the upgrade slots in each backpack or chest show what fits.

Gear
Silent GearMedium4.5/5

First goal: a Silent Gear pickaxe and sword.

Getting started

  1. Craft Blueprint Paper (paper + lapis or similar; check JEI).
  2. Craft the blueprints you want: pickaxe, sword, rod, binding. Blueprints are reusable; templates are single-use.
  3. Craft a main part (head) from your best material.
  4. Craft a rod (sticks to start, blaze rods or better later).
  5. Combine the parts in a crafting grid to make the tool or weapon.
  6. Add a binding, grip, tip or coating for extra stats and traits.
  7. Build a Material Grader and grade your ingots with a catalyst for better stats.
  8. Build a Metal Alloyer to make Crimson Steel (Nether), Azure Electrum (End) and Tyrian Steel.
  9. Craft a Salvager to break old gear into parts and recover materials.
  10. Make armor the same way: armor blueprint, main material, and a lining.
  11. Rebuild your gear every time you unlock a better material.

Tips

  • Hold Shift on a material in JEI to see its traits.
  • Broken Silent Gear tools don't vanish; repair them with repair kits.
  • Swap parts later with the Salvager to recover materials.
  • Mix two or more materials in a part to combine their traits.
  • Synergy goes down when you mix very different materials; check the tooltip.

Guide: No in-game book; JEI and tooltips show stats and traits.

ApotheosisMedium4.5/5

First goal: kill an affixed mob and salvage its gear.

Getting started

  1. Fight mobs with colored names; they carry affixed gear.
  2. Pick up and compare the gear. Affix rarity runs Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Mythic.
  3. Build a Salvaging Table and break unwanted gear into rarity materials.
  4. Build a Reforging Table and use materials to reroll affixes on your best weapon.
  5. Collect gems from mobs and chests.
  6. Build a Gem Cutting Table to upgrade gems by combining them.
  7. Socket gems into gear with sockets (use Sigils of Socketing to add sockets).
  8. Explore for boss mobs and dungeon chests for better loot.
  9. Fight bosses once your armor and weapon can handle them.

Tips

  • Bosses glow and have a boss bar. Don't fight one without good armor.
  • Apotheosis gems get stronger as you upgrade them; keep duplicates.
  • Gem dust from salvaging is used in reforging, so don't throw it away.

Guide: Use JEI and the item tooltips; affixes are listed on each item.

Just Dire ThingsEasy–Medium4/5

First goal: Ferricore from Tier 1 Goo.

Getting started

  1. Look up the first Goo tier in JEI to see how to get it.
  2. Place the Goo and set the target block next to it. It slowly converts the block; JEI shows each conversion.
  3. Collect Ferricore and craft Ferricore tools and armor.
  4. Craft JDT's early machines like the Block Breaker, Block Placer and Fuel Generator.
  5. Go to the Nether to unlock the next Goo tier and Blazegold.
  6. Go to the End to unlock Celestigem.
  7. Push to the final tier for Eclipse Alloy gear.
  8. Use the tool and armor ability menus to toggle abilities like auto-smelt or step assist.

Tips

  • JDT tools and armor have abilities you can toggle in their menu.
  • Its Fuel Generator burns any furnace fuel.
  • Goo needs time; set up several Goo blocks to speed up conversion.

Guide: Use JEI. Its Goo conversion recipes have their own JEI category.

RelicsEasy (luck-based)3.5/5

First goal: find your first relic in a loot chest.

Getting started

  1. Open loot chests in dungeons and structures.
  2. Read each relic's tooltip to see what it does.
  3. Equip relics in their slot to use their abilities.
  4. Use a relic a lot to level it up and unlock stronger abilities.
  5. Keep the relics that fit your play style and level them.

Tips

  • Each relic levels separately, so keep the ones you use most.

Magic
Iron's SpellbooksMedium4/5

First goal: a basic spellbook with one scroll in it.

Getting started

  1. Find mage towers and mob mages; they drop scrolls, Arcane Essence and gear.
  2. Craft a basic spellbook.
  3. Build an Inscription Table and put scrolls into your spellbook.
  4. Equip the spellbook and use the spell wheel key to pick a spell.
  5. Make ink and use the Scroll Forge to craft new scrolls.
  6. Build an Arcane Anvil to upgrade spell levels and combine items.
  7. Craft school armor (fire, ice, lightning, holy, etc.) to boost that school.
  8. Add rings and amulets (curios) for mana and spell power.
  9. Upgrade to a bigger spellbook for more spell slots.

Tips

  • Each school (fire, ice, lightning, holy, etc.) has its own armor and focus.
  • Mana regenerates over time; mana-boosting armor helps a lot.
  • Pick two or three spells that work together rather than many weak ones.

Guide: Use JEI and the spell tooltips.

Ars NouveauMedium4/5

First goal: a Novice Spell Book and Source Gems.

Getting started

  1. Craft the Worn Notebook and read the first chapters.
  2. Find Archwood forests for logs and Source Berries.
  3. Build an Imbuement Chamber to turn amethyst into Source Gems.
  4. Craft a Novice Spell Book.
  5. Build a Scribes Table to learn new glyphs.
  6. Make spells by combining a form (Projectile, Touch, Self) with effects.
  7. Generate Source with Sourcelinks (Volcanic, Agronomic, Mycelial, Vitalic) feeding Source Jars.
  8. Build an Enchanting Apparatus for higher-tier crafting.
  9. Upgrade to Apprentice and Archmage spell books.
  10. Make Sorcerer armor and upgrade it with threads at the Alteration Table.
  11. Summon Starbuncles to move items, and other familiars for help.

Tips

  • Combine glyphs to make your own spells.
  • Source Jars must be near Sourcelinks to fill.
  • Starbuncles are cheap, cute item transport.

Guide: Craft the Worn Notebook (book + Amethyst). It is the full guide.

Silent Gear weapon traits

  • Nickel, Invar or Barrier heads add Adamant: bonus damage against big mobs.
  • Tyrian Steel, Crimson Steel or End Rod add Sturdy: less durability loss.
  • Avoid Copper on weapons. Its Dulling trait lowers damage.
  • Run ingots through the Material Grader before crafting. Higher grades mean higher stats.

Apotheosis loop

  1. Kill affixed mobs and bosses for gear, gems and materials.
  2. Salvage gear you don't want at the Salvaging Table.
  3. Reroll your weapon at the Reforging Table.
  4. Socket gems with the Gem Cutting Table.

Enchantments Guide

Every vanilla Minecraft 1.21 enchantment, plus the modded enchantments added by mods in FTB Evolution. Each one shows what it does, its highest level, and what it goes on. Treasure enchantments can't come from an enchanting table; find them as books in loot, fishing or villager trades.

Apothic Enchanting raises the max level of most vanilla enchantments, so levels in the vanilla tables are the normal caps, not the pack's caps. The tooltip on an enchanted book always shows the real value.

How to get enchantments

  1. Build an enchanting table (book, 2 diamonds, 4 obsidian).
  2. Place 15 bookshelves around it, one block away with nothing in between. This unlocks level 30 enchants.
  3. Bring lapis lazuli and XP levels. A level-30 enchant costs 3 lapis and 3 levels, but you need 30 levels to see it.
  4. Use an anvil to combine enchanted books with gear. Two books of the same level make the next level up.
  5. Trade with Librarian villagers for exact books like Mending. Lock in a good trade by trading once.
  6. Use a grindstone to strip enchantments off unwanted gear and get some XP back.

Can't be combined

Projectile (shoots it), Touch (the block or mob you're looking at, close up), Self (you), Orbit (circles you and hits nearby mobs), Underfoot (the block under you).

Creating a Mob XP Farm

An XP farm gives you levels for enchanting and repairs, plus mob drops like bones, string and gunpowder. Pick the one that fits your stage.

StageFarmDifficultyBest for
EarlyDungeon spawner farmEasyQuick XP from a zombie or skeleton spawner you find underground
EarlyDark-room mob towerMediumXP plus every common mob drop, anywhere in the world
MidIndustrial Foregoing mob farmMediumFully automatic XP and drops of any mob you choose

Option 1: Dungeon spawner farm (early)

  1. Find a spawner. Look for mossy cobblestone rooms underground. Zombie and skeleton spawners work best.
  2. Light it up with torches on and around the spawner so it stops spawning while you build.
  3. Clear a 9×9 room around the spawner, 4 blocks tall.
  4. Place water at the back wall so it flows toward one side and pushes mobs into a 1×1 hole.
  5. Dig a drop shaft about 22 blocks down from that hole. The fall leaves mobs at 1 or 2 hearts.
  6. Build a small killing room at the bottom with a 1-block gap you can hit mobs through, and a hopper into a chest for drops.
  7. Remove the torches from the spawner. Stand within 16 blocks so it keeps spawning, and hit mobs as they land.

Option 2: Dark-room mob tower (any mob)

  1. Pick a spot over an ocean or high in the sky so mobs can't spawn anywhere else nearby.
  2. Build 3–4 floors of 2-block-tall spawning platforms, each at least 20×20, with a roof so it stays fully dark.
  3. Add water channels on each floor that flow into a center hole.
  4. Put trapdoors on the edges of the hole; mobs walk onto them and fall.
  5. Make the drop 22 blocks to a killing area with hoppers and chests.
  6. Wait 24 to 128 blocks away (in a tower or AFK spot). Mobs only spawn in that range from you, and only in total darkness.

Option 3: Industrial Foregoing mob farm (automatic)

  1. Craft a Mob Imprisonment Tool and right-click a mob to capture it.
  2. Build a Mob Duplicator, put the captured mob in it, and give it power and Essence (liquid XP).
  3. Place a Mob Crusher (or Slaughter Factory) facing the spawn area. It kills mobs and collects their XP as Essence.
  4. Pipe Essence back into the Duplicator so it keeps running, and store the extra in a tank.
  5. Collect drops with an item pipe or Refined Storage Importer into storage.
  6. Use the Essence in IF's Enchantment Applicator or Enchantment Factory to enchant items without spending your own levels.
  • Use a Looting III sword in your hand while killing mobs for more drops.
  • Endermen in the End give the most XP per kill; a simple End platform farm is a strong late upgrade.
  • Keep a Mending item in your hand or armor; XP repairs it before filling your level bar.
  • A blaze spawner in a Nether Fortress makes a great XP and blaze rod farm.

Productive Bees

Productive Bees turns bees into a farm for almost any resource: iron, gold, diamonds, osmium, redstone and more. Bees fly out, collect from flowers, and fill their hive with combs you process into materials.

DIFFICULTYMediumRATING4.5/5STARTEarly, then grow it all game

JEI is your best friend here. Productive Bees adds JEI categories for Bee Breeding, Bee Conversion, Bee Nests and Centrifuge that show exactly how to get every bee in your pack.

Getting started

  1. Craft a Bee Cage. Right-click a bee to catch it. Use a Sturdy Bee Cage later so you can catch and release many times.
  2. Catch a few normal bees from bee nests in flower forests, plains and meadows.
  3. Craft an Advanced Beehive and place it near flowers. Release your bees next to it.
  4. Add an Expansion Box on top of the hive. It holds more bees and gives upgrade slots.
  5. Collect combs. Open the hive to take Honeycombs out, or pipe them out with a hopper or Refined Storage Importer.
  6. Build a Centrifuge (later a Powered Centrifuge). It turns combs into wax, honey and the comb's resource.
  7. Find solitary bee nests. Special nests in stone, sand, snow, gravel, sugar cane, the Nether and the End spawn different bees. Craft a Nest Locator to find them.
  8. Get resource bees. Use JEI's Bee Breeding and Bee Conversion tabs. Many ore bees come from breeding two specific bees, or from converting a bee with an item.
  9. Breed bees by feeding two bees their favorite flower or Honey Treats, or put a Breeding Upgrade in the hive.
  10. Scale up. Run many hives of your best bees into Powered Centrifuges and straight into storage.

How to plan a big build

  1. Pick a theme that matches the mod: sci-fi for Mekanism, cozy wood for farms, dark stone for mob farms, colorful stone for magic.
  2. Choose 3–4 main blocks and stick to them. Builds look best with a small, matching block palette.
  3. Mark out the size with wool or dirt before you build. Leave extra room; machines and multiblocks grow.
  4. Build the shell first, then the machines, then details like lights, signs and decoration.
  5. Add depth. Push windows in and pillars out by one block so walls don't look flat.
  6. Light it well. Hide light sources in floors and ceilings so mobs can't spawn and the build glows.

Zoo-keeping basics

Moving mobs

Leads work on most animals. Boats and minecarts carry almost anything, including villagers and endermen. Buckets catch fish, axolotls and tadpoles.

Industrial Foregoing's capture tool picks up most mobs, even hostile ones, and releases them where you want. It's the best zoo tool in the pack.

Keep visitors safe

Undead burn in daylight, so roof their exhibits. Use glass and iron bars between you and hostiles, and light the paths so nothing spawns.

Tips & Tricks

Shortcuts and habits that save hours in a big pack like this one.

Base building

  • Build your base near a mix of biomes and close to a cave entrance; you will need wood, water, lava and ores all at once.
  • Leave lots of room. Multiblocks like the Fission Reactor, Induction Matrix and IE Excavator are big.
  • Keep machines in rows with a cable or conduit line behind them so you can add more later.
  • Label your storage and machines (Supplementaries signs or item frames) so you can find things fast.
  • Use Handcrafted and Supplementaries for furniture and decoration once the basics run.

Power

  • Always build more power than you need right now; every new machine adds load.
  • Add a battery (Energy Cube, Dimensional Cell or Induction Matrix) so short power dips don't stop machines.
  • Passive power (solar, wind, water wheels) keeps your base running while you are away exploring.
  • Keep Refined Storage on its own steady power source so your items never go offline.

Ore & resources

  • Double ore as early as you can. Mekanism's Enrichment Chamber or an IE Crusher pays for itself in a day.
  • Don't smelt raw ore by hand once you have a processing line. Send everything through it.
  • Use Mystical Agriculture seeds for metals you run short on, like osmium or tin.
  • Deep caves (down to Y −128) and Infinity Caves have huge ore veins; bring torches and a good pickaxe.
  • Mark big finds with a Waystone so you can come back.

Automation

  • Automate the boring stuff first: smelting, ore processing and food.
  • Set up autocrafting in Refined Storage for items you use a lot, like circuits, alloys and cables.
  • Use filters everywhere so machines only get the items they need.
  • Start simple. A pipe that works beats a clever setup you have to debug.
  • Use XNet or Integrated Dynamics later for complex item and fluid routing.

Combat

  • Upgrade armor before weapons; staying alive matters more than killing faster.
  • Carry healing food and a few potions of regeneration and fire resistance.
  • Fight Apotheosis bosses in open areas so you can back off.
  • Use a bow, crossbow or spells for dangerous mobs instead of melee.
  • Mending (or Silent Gear Renew) on your main gear means far fewer repairs.

Exploration

  • Bring a backpack with blocks, food, torches, a bed and a spare pickaxe on every trip.
  • Set your spawn with a bed before entering a new dimension.
  • Dungeon Crawl and YUNG's structures hold the best early loot; check chests before mobs.
  • Write down coordinates of anything useful: villages, dungeons, biomes with rare plants.
  • Elytra from End Cities plus fireworks or a jetpack make travel much faster.

Quests & progression

  • Claim quest rewards right away; they often include gear or materials you need for the next step.
  • Follow the quest book when you're unsure what to do next.
  • You don't have to finish every mod. Pick the paths you enjoy and come back to others later.
  • The FTB Pyramid endgame uses items from almost every mod, so build a little of each over time.

Performance & safety

  • Give the pack 8 GB of RAM in your launcher; less can cause lag and crashes.
  • Avoid huge numbers of item entities on the ground; use void upgrades or trash cans.
  • Back up your world before big changes like reactors or updating the pack.
  • If something lags, look for mob farms or giant item piles first.
  • Turn off shaders if your frame rate drops; the pack includes them, but they are heavy.
